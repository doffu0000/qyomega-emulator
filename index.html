SysEx goes to the emulated unit only. Real MIDI ports are used only through the Live MIDI card below, when you choose them.

Live MIDI

Off until you press Enable. Nothing is opened or remembered between visits. Only ports you pick here are used, and System Exclusive access is never requested from the browser.

Out sends only notes, pitch bend, aftertouch and the ordinary performance controllers. Never sent: System Exclusive (bulk dumps, resets, parameter changes), System Reset, bank select, RPN and NRPN, all sound off and the other mode controllers. Switching Out to None releases any held notes.

The small letter on each button is the computer key that presses it, and several can be held at once, so SHIFT + F1 is Shift + 1. Digits are F1 to F4 then AMP, PARAM, SONG, PATTERN, MENU, CARD; Z X C V B N M are OCT DOWN, OCT UP, TOP, BWD, FWD, STOP, REC. A mouse click while a key is held also combines (hold Shift on the keyboard, click F1).
